Grounded tailoring

Tailored, not fabricated.

SpotApply does not invent experience to match a job. It rewords what you have already done so the relevant parts are easier to find — and every changed line is checked back against your original résumé.

Original evidence

“Built FastAPI APIs supporting 50k requests/day.”

Job-specific wording

“Built production FastAPI APIs supporting 50k requests/day.”

Verified against original experience

A claim your résumé cannot support is blocked before delivery — not softened, not shipped with a warning. Tailoring that cannot be grounded does not go out.

What gets blocked

  • “Led a team of eight engineers.”

    No team size anywhere in the résumé. Blocked.

  • “Five years of Kubernetes.”

    The résumé says Kubernetes, never five years. Blocked.

  • “Reduced costs by 45%.”

    A number that appears in no bullet cannot be introduced by rewording one. Blocked.

Questions

Does SpotApply submit applications for me?

No. SpotApply prepares your documents and fills the form in your own browser. You review everything and click Submit yourself. Nothing is ever sent on your behalf.

Where do the jobs come from?

Public company career pages and ATS providers — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Personio, Rippling, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Breezy, Pinpoint and join.com — polled continuously.

How does the match score work?

Each role is read against your résumé and scored 0–100 on skills, experience, seniority and eligibility. Only roles scoring 70 or above reach your board, and each one carries the reasoning behind its score.

Will the tailored résumé say things I did not do?

No. Tailoring only rewords evidence already in your résumé, and every changed line is verified against the original. Anything unsupported is blocked rather than shipped. The output is ATS-safe and keyword-aligned — which is not the same as a guarantee that any particular system or recruiter will advance you.

I need visa sponsorship — does that help or hurt?

It is part of the score. Roles that state they will not sponsor are filtered out, and cap-exempt and known-sponsor employers are surfaced, so your board reflects where you can actually be hired.

What happens to my résumé?

It is stored privately to your account and used only to match and prepare your own applications. It is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising. You can replace or delete it at any time.
