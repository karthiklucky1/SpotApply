Grounded tailoring

Tailored, not fabricated.

SpotApply does not invent experience to match a job. It rewords what you have already done so a screener finds the relevant parts faster. Every changed line is then checked back against your original.

Original evidence

“Built FastAPI APIs supporting 50k requests/day.”

Job-specific wording

“Built production FastAPI APIs supporting 50k requests/day.”

Verified against original experience

A claim your resume cannot support is blocked before delivery. It is not softened, and it does not ship with a warning attached.

What gets blocked

  • “Led a team of eight engineers.”

    No team size anywhere in the resume. Blocked.

  • “Five years of Kubernetes.”

    The resume says Kubernetes, never five years. Blocked.

  • “Reduced costs by 45%.”

    A number that appears in no bullet cannot be introduced by rewording one. Blocked.

Questions

Does SpotApply submit applications for me?

No. SpotApply prepares your documents and fills the form in your own browser. You review everything and click Submit yourself. Nothing is ever sent on your behalf.

Where do the jobs come from?

Two ways. Straight from the ATS a company hires on: Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Personio, Rippling, BambooHR, Teamtailor, Recruitee, Breezy, Pinpoint and join.com — which is where a posting appears first. And from public job feeds and aggregators, including Remotive, RemoteOK, The Muse, WeWorkRemotely, Arbeitnow, Jobicy, Adzuna, Reed, Jooble, Hacker News Who's Hiring, Y Combinator, Google Jobs and LinkedIn. 32 providers in total, across more than 50,000 company boards polled continuously.

How does the match score work?

Each role is read against your resume and scored 0–100 on skills, experience, seniority and eligibility. Only roles scoring 70 or above reach your board, and each one carries the reasoning behind its score.

Will the tailored resume say things I did not do?

No. Tailoring only rewords evidence already in your resume, and every changed line is verified against the original. Anything unsupported is blocked rather than shipped. The output is ATS-safe and keyword-aligned. That is not the same as a promise that any particular system or recruiter will move you forward, and we will not pretend otherwise.

I need visa sponsorship — does that help or hurt?

It is part of the score. Roles that state they will not sponsor are filtered out, and cap-exempt and known-sponsor employers are surfaced, so your board reflects where you can actually be hired.

What happens to my resume?

It is stored privately to your account and used only to match and prepare your own applications. It is never sold or shared with third parties for advertising. You can replace or delete it at any time.
