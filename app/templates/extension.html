SpotApply Extension

Auto-fill job forms in your browser — Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday and more. You review every field and click Submit yourself. On LinkedIn and Indeed, those sites fill your details from your own account, so SpotApply only tracks the application there.

Add to Chrome (free)

Installs from the Chrome Web Store and keeps itself up to date. Works in Chrome and other Chromium browsers (Edge, Brave, Arc).

Install in Chrome (3 steps)

1

Click "Add to Chrome" above

It opens SpotApply's page on the Chrome Web Store.

2

Click "Add to Chrome", then "Add extension"

Chrome asks you to confirm. That's it: no downloads or developer settings.

3

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle icon next to the address bar and pin SpotApply, then open your SpotApply dashboard once so it connects.

Installed the .zip version before?

Remove that copy first: open chrome://extensions and click Remove on the old SpotApply. With two copies, both would open and fill the same form. The Web Store version updates itself from now on.

How to use

1️⃣ Open your SpotApply dashboard and pick a job
2️⃣ Click Auto-Fill & Apply. The application form opens in a new tab and fills itself
3️⃣ If a form isn't filled, click the SpotApply icon in your toolbar, then ⚡ Fill This Form Now
4️⃣ Review every field, then click Submit yourself (or turn on One-click submit in Settings and your Apply & Submit click does it on boards that allow it)
Developer install (unpacked)

Only for testing a build that isn't on the Chrome Web Store yet. Remove the Web Store copy first.

  1. Download the .zip and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
